HAMZA FAMILY CINEMA - how to put it online (free, about 2 minutes)

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (free).
2. Drag this whole "site" folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a link.
3. Send the link to your family.

To add or change videos later:
- Edit library.js (any text editor), then drag the folder onto your site's
  "Deploys" page in Netlify to update it.

Video tips:
- Convert to MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio (HandBrake, "Fast 1080p30" preset).
- In Drive, set sharing to "Anyone with the link" -> Viewer.
- Wait a few minutes after uploading before testing.
